How to play

Both fighters choose a move at the same time, then the turn plays out frame by frame. There are no reflexes here — only the guess, and what you know about the person guessing back.

The threat board

This is the part the original game makes you work out for yourself. Hover any move and the panel on the right runs every reply your rival currently has, simulates it against your move, and ranks the results. The top row is their best answer. The bar on each button is the share of their options that beat it.

Turn the Oracle down to Risk only or Off in the header once you want the guessing back.

What beats what

  • Speed decides trades. A 4-frame Jab beats a 6-frame Grab, every time.
  • High Guard stops highs and mids; Sweep goes under it.
  • Low Guard stops lows and mids; Overhead comes over it.
  • Grab ignores both guards and the Parry — but loses to anything faster.
  • Parry is live on frames 2–7 only. It eats fast moves and misses slow ones.
  • Dodge Roll is invulnerable and retreats. Only an armoured Lunge chases it down.
  • Lunge tanks one strike on the way in, and gets stuffed by guards and grabs.

Resources

  • Meter (the gold pips) buys Rising Fang, an invulnerable reversal. Hustle banks two, if they let you.
  • Burst (the blue bar) escapes a combo — unless they bait it by guarding.
  • Sadness: refuse to engage and the ring starts taking health off you.
  • At turn 60 it is time over, and whoever is ahead on health takes it.
