Pick your fighter

Three archetypes, one shared defensive skeleton. Guards, Parry, Dodge, movement and Burst work the same for everyone — what changes is where you sit on the speed, reach and damage triangle.

How to play

Both fighters choose a move at the same time, then the turn plays out frame by frame. There are no reflexes here — only the guess, and what you know about the person guessing back. First to two rounds wins.

The threat board

This is the part the original game makes you work out for yourself. Hover any move and the panel on the right runs every reply your rival currently has, simulates it against your move, and ranks the results. The top row is their best answer. The bar on each button is the share of their options that beat it.

The yomi chain

Switch the Oracle to Yomi chain and it stops showing a list and starts showing a ladder: what beats your move, what beats that, and what beats that. Layer 1 is the obvious answer. Layer 2 is what to play if you think they have read you. The chain ends where the read loops — that is the point where thinking deeper stops helping and it is a coin flip again.

After each exchange, the line under the arena tells you what would actually have worked.

What beats what

  • Speed decides trades. A 4-frame Jab beats a 6-frame Grab, every time.
  • Counter-hits. Catch someone in their wind-up and it hurts 40% more.
  • High Guard stops highs and mids; lows go under it.
  • Low Guard stops lows and mids; highs come over it.
  • Grabs ignore both guards and the Parry — but lose to anything faster.
  • Parry is live on frames 2–9 only. It eats fast moves and misses slow ones.
  • Dodge Roll is invulnerable and retreats. Chasing moves still catch it.
  • Armour tanks one strike on the way in, at the cost of some of your own damage. Grabs go straight through it.

Resources

  • Meter (gold pips) buys your super. Hustle banks two, if they let you.
  • Burst (the blue bar) escapes a combo — unless they bait it by guarding.
  • Sadness: refuse to engage and the ring starts taking health off you.
  • At turn 70 it is time over, and whoever leads on health takes the round.
