How to play & controls

The loop

Pick a troop once — your crystal keeps recruiting it until the gold runs out. Press it again to stop and bank, or pick another to switch. Shatter the enemy crystal to win.

Armour is the counter system

Every troop is LIGHT or ARMORED and carries separate damage against each; armour subtracts flat damage on top. Light-killers butcher fodder and cannot dent plate. Armour-breakers are the reverse. The bar under each card scores that troop against the enemy's army right now.

Your army is your economy

Every living troop pays income at each payday. Hold the shrine mid-field for a bonus. Champions are limited to one on the field at a time.
