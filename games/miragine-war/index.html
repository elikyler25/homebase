MIRAGE WAR

Two players, one keyboard, one screen. No turns and no waiting — buy troops whenever you can afford them and they march out of your crystal immediately. Smash the enemy crystal to win.

The counter system is armour. Every unit is LIGHT or ARMORED, and every unit has separate damage against each. A Ninja butchers light troops and barely scratches armour. A Monk is the reverse. Armour also subtracts flat damage, so cheap swarms literally cannot hurt an Iron Knight — but a Vampire drains it.

Your standing army is your economy. Every living unit pays income at each payday, so units you keep alive fund the next wave. Trading badly loses you the game twice over.
