MIRAGE WAR

Buy troops whenever you can afford them — they march out of your crystal immediately. No turns, no waiting. Shatter the enemy crystal to win. Crystals shoot back at anything that closes.

Armour is the counter system. Every unit is LIGHT or ARMORED and has separate damage against each, and armour subtracts flat damage on top. A Ninja butchers light troops and cannot dent plate; a Monk is the reverse. Cheap swarms literally cannot hurt an Iron Knight — a Vampire drains it dry. Your standing army is your economy: every living unit pays income at each payday.
