Slot Racer

Your car is pinned in a groove, so there is no steering. One button: hold it for power, let go and the motor brakes. Everything is in when you lift.

How to be quick

Watch the back of your car. The guide pin holds the nose in the slot, so as a corner loads up the tail steps out — and how far it is thrown is exactly how close the pin is to popping. That is the instrument.

The bar across the top says the same thing in numbers, and the amber marker on it is what the corner ahead will ask for if you keep your thumb down. When it runs past the end, lift.

You cannot see far enough down the road to guess, and by the time a corner is loading the car it is too late to fix. The cue is the game: lift when it lights, get back on the moment it clears.

Push past the line and the guide pin pops out of the slot: you are off, and a marshal takes 2.4 seconds to put you back. One deslot costs more than a whole lap of being careful.
